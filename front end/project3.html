biriyani menu

biryani is a famous and flavorful rice dish from the Wikipedia made with basmati rice, aromatic spices, and meat or vegetables.About BiryaniBiryani is a popular mixed rice dish enjoyed across the world. It uses fragrant basmati rice cooked with rich spices like cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon. Common varieties include chicken biryani, mutton biryani, and vegetarian options.The ingredients and cooking methods vary by region, such as Hyderabadi or Kolkata styles. It is often served hot with cooling raita or gravy.People love preparing this special dish during festivals and family gatherings.
